# AviQuest Social Media Content Plan

## Brand Guidelines for Social

### Voice
- Enthusiastic, never over-the-top
- Knowledgeable, never condescending
- Playful, never childish
- Nature-loving without being preachy

### Visual Style
- Dark forest green (#0A1F0F) and amber (#FFC107) as primary brand colors (matching the app UI)
- Nature photography with overlay text in brand fonts
- Consistent template designs for recurring content series
- Species images from Wikimedia Commons (with attribution)

### Hashtag Strategy
**Primary (always use)**: #AviQuest #BirdingAdventure
**Secondary (rotate)**: #BirdWatching #Birding #BirdsOfInstagram #NatureLovers #WildlifePhotography #BirdID
**Content-specific**: #BirdOfTheWeek #LegendaryBird #RareBird #BirdFacts #BirdCalls
**Gaming**: #MobileGaming #CollectionGame #IndieGame #GamifiedNature

## Content Series

### 1. Bird of the Week

**Format**: Single image or carousel post
**Frequency**: Every Monday
**Platform**: Instagram (feed), X/Twitter, Facebook

**Template**:
```
BIRD OF THE WEEK

[Bird Name]
Scientific name: [Scientific Name]
Rarity: [Common/Uncommon/Rare/Legendary]
Habitat: [Habitat]
Conservation: [Status]

[Interesting lore/fact from the app]

Have you spotted this one in AviQuest? Drop a [emoji] if it's in your aviary!

#AviQuest #BirdOfTheWeek #BirdWatching #[BirdName]
```

**Example Posts**:

**Week 1 - Resplendent Quetzal (Legendary)**
> BIRD OF THE WEEK: Resplendent Quetzal
>
> This legendary Central American bird was sacred to the Aztecs and Maya — they considered killing one a capital crime. Its tail feathers can grow over 60cm long.
>
> In AviQuest, the Quetzal is one of our rarest Legendary species (3% spawn rate) worth 500 XP. Only the most dedicated birders will add this one to their aviary.
>
> #AviQuest #BirdOfTheWeek #Quetzal #LegendaryBird #CloudForest

**Week 2 - Black-capped Chickadee (Common)**
> BIRD OF THE WEEK: Black-capped Chickadee
>
> This cheerful little bird remembers exactly who feeds it and can recall thousands of food cache locations. It's basically the bird world's memory champion.
>
> One of the first species most AviQuest players collect. Common but never boring — worth 50 XP.
>
> #AviQuest #BirdOfTheWeek #Chickadee #BackyardBirding

### 2. Did You Know? (Bird Facts)

**Format**: Short text post or 15-second video
**Frequency**: Wednesday and Friday
**Platform**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, X/Twitter

**Template**:
```
DID YOU KNOW?

[Surprising bird fact pulled from app lore]

Learn more about [Bird Name] in AviQuest — link in bio.

#BirdFacts #AviQuest #NatureTok
```

**Example Posts**:

> DID YOU KNOW? Rock Pigeons were domesticated over 5,000 years ago and have won military medals for bravery. Yes, bird war heroes.
>
> They're a Common species in AviQuest worth 20 XP. Don't sleep on the pigeons.

> DID YOU KNOW? European Starlings can mimic phones, other birds, and even human speech. Their flocks (murmurations) can involve millions of birds creating shapes in the sky.
>
> Spot one in AviQuest and earn 30 XP.

### 3. Rarity Reveal

**Format**: Short video with build-up and reveal
**Frequency**: Every other Tuesday
**Platform**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts

**Concept**: Build suspense around discovering a bird, then reveal its rarity tier with color-coded effects (white for Common, green for Uncommon, blue for Rare, amber/gold for Legendary).

**Script Template**:
```
[Scene: Phone pointed at nature]
Text: "Just spotted something..."

[Scene: App capture animation]
Text: "Please be rare... please be rare..."

[Scene: Rarity reveal with color flash]
Text: "[LEGENDARY] [Bird Name]! [XP] XP!"

[Scene: Celebration]
Text: "Added to the aviary!"

Audio: Trending sound or bird call
```

### 6. Conservation Corner

**Format**: Long caption with image or carousel
**Frequency**: Last Sunday of each month
**Platform**: Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter

**Template**:
```
CONSERVATION CORNER

[Species Name] — [Conservation Status]

[2-3 paragraphs about the species' conservation situation, written accessibly]

In AviQuest, [Species Name] is classified as [Rarity] — a reminder that rarity in our game reflects real-world scarcity.

What can you do?
- [Actionable tip 1]
- [Actionable tip 2]
- [Link to conservation organization]

#Conservation #EndangeredSpecies #AviQuest #ProtectBirds
```

**Example**:
> CONSERVATION CORNER: Kakapo
>
> Status: Critically Endangered
>
> The Kakapo is the world's only flightless parrot. Fewer than 250 exist in the wild, all on predator-free island sanctuaries in New Zealand. Each one has a name. Each one matters.
>
> In AviQuest, the Kakapo is a Legendary species worth 800 XP — our highest-value bird. Its rarity in-game mirrors its rarity in nature.
>
> Want to help? Support the Kakapo Recovery Programme: kakaporecovery.org.nz

## Platform-Specific Strategies

### Instagram
- **Feed**: 3x/week (Mon: Bird of the Week, Thu: Birding Tips carousel, Sat: player feature or conservation)
- **Stories**: Daily (polls, quizzes, behind-the-scenes, quick facts)
- **Reels**: 2x/week (Rarity Reveals, Did You Know)
- **Story Highlights**: Bird of the Week, Legendary Birds, Birding Tips, Achievements, Community

### TikTok
- **Posts**: 4–5x/week
- **Content mix**: 40% bird facts (Did You Know), 30% rarity reveals/gameplay, 20% birding tips, 10% trending sound adaptations
- **Comment strategy**: Reply to every comment in first hour; pin best comment
- **Duet/Stitch**: Engage with other nature creators

### X/Twitter
- **Tweets**: 1–2x daily
- **Content mix**: Quick bird facts, engagement questions, dev updates, retweets of bird content
- **Threads**: Weekly deep dives on species or game features
- **Quote tweets**: React to trending nature/bird content

**Engagement Tweet Templates**:
- "Name a bird. Any bird. We'll tell you its rarity in AviQuest."
- "What's the first bird you ever noticed and thought 'that's actually cool'?"
- "Controversial take: pigeons are underrated."
- "Your last AviQuest catch is now your pet. What is it?"

### YouTube
- **Long-form**: 1x/week (species deep dives, birding guides, development vlogs)
- **Shorts**: 3x/week (repurposed TikTok/Reels content)
- **Titles**: SEO-optimized with keywords ("Top 10 Rarest Birds", "Bird Identification for Beginners")
- **Thumbnails**: Consistent template with bird image + bold text + rarity color border

### Pinterest
- **Pins**: 5x/week
- **Boards**: "Bird Identification Guide", "Birding Tips for Beginners", "Rare & Legendary Birds", "Backyard Birding", "Bird Photography"
- **Content**: Infographic-style bird fact cards, birding tip visuals, species comparison graphics

## Engagement Campaigns

### Campaign 1: "#MyFirstBird"
- **Concept**: Ask users to share their first AviQuest capture
- **Mechanic**: Post your first bird screenshot with #MyFirstBird #AviQuest
- **Duration**: Ongoing
- **Goal**: UGC generation, app awareness

### Campaign 2: "Legendary Hunt"
- **Concept**: Weekly challenge to find specific legendary species
- **Mechanic**: First players to screenshot finding the featured legendary get featured on social
- **Duration**: Recurring weekly event
- **Goal**: Engagement, daily active users

### Campaign 3: "Birder Bingo"
- **Concept**: Monthly bingo card with species to find
- **Mechanic**: Share a bingo card graphic. Players screenshot completed rows/cards.
- **Duration**: Monthly
- **Goal**: Retention, collection completionism

### Campaign 4: "Spot & Share" Photo Challenge
- **Concept**: Monthly real-world bird photography challenge
- **Mechanic**: Take a photo of a real bird, identify it in AviQuest, share both side-by-side
- **Duration**: Monthly
- **Goal**: Bridge game and real birding, generate authentic content

## Content Production Workflow

### Weekly Schedule
| Day | Content | Platform | Notes |
|-----|---------|----------|-------|
| Mon | Bird of the Week | Instagram, X, Facebook | Pre-scheduled |
| Tue | Rarity Reveal (bi-weekly) | TikTok, Reels | Video production |
| Wed | Did You Know | TikTok, X | Quick production |
| Thu | Birding Tips | Instagram carousel, Pinterest | Carousel design |
| Fri | Did You Know | TikTok, Reels, X | Quick production |
| Sat | Community/UGC feature | Instagram, X | Curate submissions |
| Sun | Conservation Corner (monthly) / Engagement post | Instagram, Facebook | Long-form when monthly |

### Tools
- **Design**: Canva Pro (templates, carousels, stories)
- **Scheduling**: Buffer or Later (multi-platform scheduling)
- **Video editing**: CapCut (TikTok/Reels), DaVinci Resolve (YouTube)
- **Analytics**: Native platform analytics + Google Analytics for link tracking
- **UGC management**: Branded hashtag monitoring via social listening tools
