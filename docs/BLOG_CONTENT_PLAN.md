# AviQuest Blog & SEO Content Plan

## Blog Strategy Overview

The AviQuest blog serves as the primary SEO engine and content hub. It targets three keyword clusters: bird identification (high volume), birding for beginners (medium volume), and species-specific queries (long tail). Every blog post should include a clear CTA to download AviQuest.

---

## Content Categories

### Category 1: Species Spotlights
**SEO target**: "[bird name] facts", "[bird name] habitat", "[bird name] interesting facts"
**Word count**: 1,000–1,500 words
**Format**: Educational article with images, embedded bird call audio, and species data card

### Category 2: Beginner Guides
**SEO target**: "birding for beginners", "how to identify birds", "bird watching tips"
**Word count**: 1,500–2,500 words
**Format**: How-to guide with numbered steps, images, and practical advice

### Category 3: Listicles & Roundups
**SEO target**: "rarest birds in the world", "best birding apps", "most beautiful birds"
**Word count**: 1,500–2,000 words
**Format**: Numbered list with images and descriptions for each entry

### Category 4: Seasonal & Timely
**SEO target**: "spring bird migration [year]", "winter bird feeding", "birds in [month]"
**Word count**: 1,000–1,500 words
**Format**: Seasonal guide updated annually for freshness

### Category 5: Dev Blog / Behind the Build
**SEO target**: "Flutter app development", "gamification design", "indie app development"
**Word count**: 1,000–2,000 words
**Format**: Technical article with code snippets, design decisions, and learnings

## SEO Technical Requirements

### On-Page SEO Checklist
- [ ] Target keyword in title (H1), first paragraph, and at least one H2
- [ ] Meta title under 60 characters with keyword
- [ ] Meta description under 155 characters with keyword and CTA
- [ ] At least 3 internal links to other blog posts or app pages
- [ ] Alt text on all images with descriptive, keyword-relevant text
- [ ] At least one external link to authoritative source (Cornell, Audubon, IUCN)
- [ ] Table of contents for posts over 1,500 words
- [ ] Schema markup: Article schema for blog posts, FAQ schema where applicable
- [ ] Mobile-friendly formatting (short paragraphs, subheadings every 200-300 words)
- [ ] CTA to download AviQuest in intro and conclusion

### Internal Linking Strategy
- Species spotlight posts link to related beginner guides
- Beginner guides link to relevant species spotlights as examples
- All posts link to a central "Download AviQuest" page
- Listicles link to individual species spotlight posts for items that have them
- Seasonal posts link to relevant species and beginner content

### Content Update Schedule
- Seasonal posts: Update annually before the relevant season
- Listicles: Review and update every 6 months
- Species spotlights: Update conservation status annually
- Beginner guides: Review for accuracy every 12 months

## Content Distribution Plan (Per Blog Post)

### Day of Publish
1. Publish blog post with full SEO optimization
2. Share on X/Twitter with key quote or stat
3. Post Instagram Story with link
4. Share in relevant Facebook groups (if allowed)
5. Send to email subscribers (if part of newsletter cycle)

### Day 2-3
6. Create Instagram carousel summarizing key points
7. Post TikTok/Reel with one compelling fact from the post
8. Share on Reddit in relevant subreddit (value-first, not promotional)

### Day 4-7
9. Create Pinterest pin with blog graphic
10. Repurpose into X/Twitter thread
11. Cross-post to Dev.to or Hashnode (dev blog posts only)

### Day 14-30
12. Boost top-performing social posts with paid promotion
13. Pitch for inclusion in relevant newsletters or roundups
14. Monitor search rankings and adjust meta/content if needed

## Measurement & Optimization

### Per-Post Metrics
- Organic search impressions and clicks (Google Search Console)
- Page views and unique visitors (GA4)
- Average time on page
- Bounce rate
- Scroll depth
- CTA click-through rate (app store link clicks)
- Social shares

### Monthly Blog Metrics
- Total organic traffic
- New vs. returning visitors
- Top-performing posts by traffic
- Top-performing posts by engagement (time on page)
- Keyword ranking movements
- Backlinks acquired

### Quarterly Review
- Content audit: which posts are performing, which need updates
- Keyword gap analysis: what competitors rank for that we don't
- Content calendar adjustment based on performance data
- New topic opportunities from search trend analysis
