### Full Description (4,000 characters max)

Turn every walk into a birding adventure.

AviQuest is the bird identification app that feels like a game. Point your camera at a bird, add it to your collection, earn XP, and progress through 20+ levels as you build the ultimate aviary. With 393 real bird species, authentic bird calls, and a rarity system that keeps every encounter exciting, AviQuest makes birdwatching accessible and rewarding for everyone.

DISCOVER 393 REAL BIRD SPECIES
Explore a database of birds from every continent. Each species includes scientific classification, habitat information, conservation status, and unique lore that brings every bird to life. From the common House Sparrow to the legendary Resplendent Quetzal, every discovery matters.

FOUR RARITY TIERS
Not all birds are created equal. Species are classified across four rarity tiers — Common (60%), Uncommon (25%), Rare (12%), and Legendary (3%). Legendary birds like the Shoebill, Kakapo, and Harpy Eagle are the ultimate prize, awarding 5x XP when discovered.

LEVEL UP YOUR BIRDING SKILLS
Start as a Fledgling and earn your way to Master Birder. The progression system tracks your XP, maintains daily streaks, and unlocks rank titles as you level up: Nestling, Sparrow, Warbler, Songweaver, Falconer, Eagle Scout, and Master Birder.

UNLOCK ACHIEVEMENTS
Earn badges for milestones like your First Feather, discovering 5 species (Nature Curious), finding a rare bird (Rare Encounter), or spotting a legendary species (Legend Spotter). Nine achievements challenge you to explore further.

LISTEN TO REAL BIRD CALLS
Every species with available recordings includes authentic bird calls sourced from Xeno-Canto, the world's largest collection of bird sounds. Learn to identify birds by sound as well as sight.

BROWSE THE BIRD GUIDE
Search and filter all 393 species by name or rarity. The built-in encyclopedia gives you instant access to habitat, conservation data, and more — whether you're in the field or on the couch.

BUILT FOR BIRD LOVERS
AviQuest combines the thrill of collection-based games with genuine ornithological data. No ads. No paywalls. Just birds.

Download AviQuest and start your birding adventure today.

## App Store Optimization (ASO) Notes

### Screenshot Strategy (Recommended Order)
1. **Hero shot**: App main screen with bird collection visible. Headline: "Your Birding Adventure Starts Here"
2. **Camera capture**: Bird identification in action. Headline: "Point. Identify. Collect."
3. **Rarity system**: Show all four tiers with example birds. Headline: "393 Species. 4 Rarity Tiers."
4. **Progression**: Level/XP screen with rank title. Headline: "From Fledgling to Master Birder"
5. **Achievements**: Achievement grid with unlocked badges. Headline: "Unlock 9 Achievements"
6. **Bird guide**: Encyclopedia view with search. Headline: "The Complete Bird Guide"
7. **Bird detail**: Species card with lore and audio. Headline: "Real Data. Real Bird Calls."
8. **Legendary reveal**: Legendary bird discovery moment. Headline: "Can You Find the Legendaries?"

### A/B Test Ideas
- **Icon**: Bird silhouette vs. illustrated bird vs. binoculars
- **Screenshot order**: Lead with gameplay vs. lead with bird beauty
- **Short description**: Gamification angle vs. bird ID utility angle
- **Feature graphic**: Adventure theme vs. nature photography style
