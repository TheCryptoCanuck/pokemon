## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Hound turns every dog encounter into a discovery. Point your camera at any dog and instantly identify its breed using on-device AI -- no internet required, no photos uploaded.

IDENTIFY WITH CONFIDENCE
Snap a photo or pick one from your gallery. Hound's machine learning model recognizes 150 dog breeds in under a second, running entirely on your phone. Your photos never leave your device.

COLLECT THEM ALL
Build your personal breed collection as you discover new dogs in the wild. Track your progress across 150 breeds organized by rarity -- from common Golden Retrievers to legendary Azawakhs.

GAMIFICATION THAT KEEPS YOU EXPLORING
- Earn XP for every identification and level up your trainer profile
- Maintain daily streaks and discovery combos for bonus rewards
- Complete daily challenges and flash challenges for extra XP
- Unlock achievements like breed set completions and mastery milestones
- Take breed group exams to earn Bronze, Silver, and Gold certifications

LEARN ABOUT EVERY BREED
Browse the Field Guide with detailed information on each breed -- temperament, origin, AKC group, size, and fun facts. Each breed card shows everything a dog lover wants to know.

LOST DOG NETWORK
Report a missing dog or help reunite lost dogs with their owners. Browse active reports on a live map, scan strays to check against reports, and get alerts for dogs spotted near you.

YOUR DOG'S PROFILE
Create profiles for your own dogs, track their adventures, and share a digital Dog Passport card with fellow dog lovers.

PRIVACY FIRST
All breed identification happens on your device using TensorFlow Lite. No photos are uploaded. No account required to start scanning -- just open the app and go.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- 150 recognizable dog breeds (more coming soon)
- On-device AI identification (works offline)
- Personal breed collection with rarity tiers
- XP, levels, streaks, combos, and achievements
- Breed group certification exams
- Detailed Field Guide
- Lost dog reporting and alerts
- Live neighborhood map
- Dog profiles and passport cards
- Demo mode to explore without scanning

Download Hound and start discovering the dogs around you.
