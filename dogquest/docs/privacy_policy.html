Hound Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Thank you for using Hound. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our dog identification app.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect the following information:

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password (stored securely using one-way hashing)
  • Dog collection data (breeds identified, sighting history)
  • Location data (GPS coordinates) when you identify a breed, if you have granted location permission
  • Basic device information (OS version, app version) for troubleshooting

2. Camera Usage

Hound requests camera access solely for the purpose of dog breed identification. Photos taken through the app are processed entirely on your device. Images are NOT uploaded to any server or shared with any third party. After identification is complete, the photo data remains on your device only.

3. On-Device Machine Learning

Dog identification is powered by a TensorFlow Lite model that runs entirely on your device. No images or sensor data are sent to external services for identification purposes. All inference happens locally, ensuring your photos never leave your phone.

3a. Location Data

When you grant location permission, Hound records the GPS coordinates of each breed identification. This data is stored locally on your device and is used to:

  • Show your sightings on a map
  • Enable location-based achievements and challenges

4. Data Storage

App data such as your dog collection, sighting history, XP progress, and achievements are stored locally on your device using an on-device database. If you create an account, your collection data is synced to our backend server so you can restore your progress if you switch devices.

5. Third-Party Services

Hound uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google) to understand app usage patterns and diagnose crashes. This includes anonymous usage events, crash reports, and basic device information.
  • AdMob (Google) to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.
  • Supabase for account authentication and data sync when you create an account. Your email and collection data are stored on Supabase servers.
  • Wikimedia Commons for dog breed reference images. Your IP address may be visible to image hosting servers.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data (name, email, password) to third parties.

6a. Aggregated Sighting Data (Opt-In)

If you opt in to "Contribute to Science" in Settings, your species observation data may be shared in aggregated, de-identified form with conservation organizations, researchers, environmental consulting firms, and government agencies studying wildlife populations and biodiversity.

Shared data includes: species name, date/time, GPS coordinates, and identification confidence. It does NOT include your name, email, username, photos, or audio recordings. You can opt out at any time in Settings, and future sightings will no longer be included.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete your local app data at any time by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app. To request deletion of your account and all associated server-side data, please contact us at the email address below. We will process your request within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Hound does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
